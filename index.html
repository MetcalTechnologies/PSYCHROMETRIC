Wet bulb here is the thermodynamic wet bulb temperature. Humidity ratio, specific volume and enthalpy are all per kilogram of dry air, which is the convention in the ASHRAE tables and on a psychrometric chart. Barometric pressure matters: at 101.325 kPa the values match sea level, and a chamber at altitude or a different site pressure will shift the humidity ratio and dew point.
